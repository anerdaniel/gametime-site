Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ADAA, CORP. (“ADAA,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information in connection with the GAME TIME mobile application and this website (together, the “Services”). GAME TIME is a collection of classic board games, including Backgammon, Chess, Sudoku, and Mahjong.

The data controller is ADAA, CORP., 3498 NE Fourth Ave, Boca Raton, FL 33431, USA. You can reach us at playtime.gtc@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect:

  • Account information. If you create an account, we may collect identifiers such as a display name, email address, and an account identifier. You may sign in with Google or Apple. When you do, we receive information those providers share with us, which typically includes a name or identifier and an email address (Apple may hide your email behind a relay address if you choose that option).
  • Profile, progress, and gameplay data. We store game progress, preferences, match history, and similar profile information so you can continue playing across sessions and, where available, play online matches with other users.
  • Online play information. If you use online features, we process the information needed to run matches, invitations, presence, and related communications inside the app.
  • Device and diagnostics information. We may collect device type, operating system, app version, language, and crash or performance logs.
  • Usage information. We may collect limited analytics about how the app is used, such as feature engagement and approximate session activity, to improve the Services.
  • Support communications. If you email us, we collect the content of your message and the contact details you provide.

You can use some parts of the app without creating an account, but certain features — including cloud save, profiles, and online play — require one.

2. How we use Firebase and other service providers

We use Google Firebase and related Google Cloud services to operate the Services, which may include:

  • Firebase Authentication, including Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple, to create and manage accounts;
  • Cloud Firestore to store profiles, progress, and online match data; and
  • Firebase Crashlytics and/or Google Analytics for Firebase, if enabled, to understand crashes, stability, and how the app is used.

These providers process information on our behalf to host and operate the Services. Their own privacy terms also apply to their processing. We do not use the Services to show you third-party advertising, and this website does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • provide, maintain, and improve the Services, including accounts, saved progress, and online play;
  • authenticate you and keep your account secure;
  • diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, and fix bugs;
  • understand usage so we can improve features and performance;
  • respond to support and privacy requests; and
  • comply with law and enforce our terms.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We may share information:

  • with service providers who help us operate the Services, such as Google (Firebase and related services), under contracts that limit their use of the data;
  • with other players, only as needed for online play (for example, a display name or public profile information and match-related data);
  • if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of ADAA, our users, or the public; and
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, or other corporate transaction, subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Retention

We keep account, profile, and gameplay information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Services. We may retain limited records after deletion where we must do so for security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance, and then delete or de-identify them when those needs end. Crash and analytics data, when collected, are kept only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes.

6. Account deletion and your choices

You may request deletion of your GAME TIME account and associated personal data:

  • in the app, through the account or settings screens when that option is available; or
  • by emailing playtime.gtc@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, and asking us to delete it.

We will process verified deletion requests within a reasonable time. Some information may remain in backups for a limited period or as required by law. You may also disconnect Google or Apple sign-in from those providers’ account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, or export personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email us at the address above.

7. Children’s privacy

GAME TIME is a general-audience board-games app. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at playtime.gtc@gmail.com. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it and close the associated account.

If we later offer a version of the Services intended for children, we will update this policy and apply any additional parental-consent and privacy requirements that apply.

8. Security
